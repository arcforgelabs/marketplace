# Canon — the lineage you reason from

Who taught what, and what to steal. Read for instinct, not citation. Links
checked 2026-06-21.

- **Dieter Rams** — "less, but better"; good design is unobtrusive and as little
  design as possible. *Steal:* the courage to remove. https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design
- **Naoto Fukasawa / Jasper Morrison — Super Normal** — design that dissolves into
  behaviour. *Steal:* for utilities, invisibility is the win.
- **Massimo Vignelli — The Vignelli Canon** — discipline; few typefaces. *Steal:*
  a tiny palette done perfectly beats variety done loosely. https://www.rit.edu/vignellicenter/sites/rit.edu.vignellicenter/files/documents/The%20Vignelli%20Canon.pdf
- **Josef Müller-Brockmann — Grid Systems** — the grid as rhythm. *Steal:*
  everything on a consistent rhythm; it's not optional.
- **Don Norman — The Design of Everyday Things** — affordances, signifiers,
  feedback. *Steal:* if users can't tell what's clickable or what just happened,
  it's broken. https://jnd.org/
- **Bret Victor — Magic Ink** — interactivity is often a crutch. *Steal:* ask
  whether the right *view* removes the need for the control. https://worrydream.com/MagicInk/
- **Elon Musk — the 5-step algorithm** — (1) question every requirement, (2)
  delete the part/step, (3) optimise, (4) accelerate, (5) automate — in that order,
  never backwards. *Steal:* don't optimise (or automate) something that should not
  exist; subtraction precedes polish. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jgw-hlFQk4
- **Edward Tufte — The Visual Display of Quantitative Information** — data-ink,
  small multiples. *Steal:* density is calm when it's all signal. https://www.edwardtufte.com/
- **Josef Albers — Interaction of Color** — color is relative. *Steal:* judge
  every color in context and in both themes.
- **Thomas & Johnston — The Illusion of Life (Disney's 12 principles)** — easing,
  anticipation, staging. *Steal:* eased motion feels alive; linear is dead.
- **Apple — Designing Fluid Interfaces (WWDC18, 803)** — interruptible, spring
  motion. *Steal:* motion responds to input mid-flight. https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2018/803/
- **Wathan & Schoger — Refactoring UI** — the practical workhorse. *Steal:*
  hierarchy via weight/size/value not borders; a spacing scale; one accent. https://refactoringui.com/
- **Jon Yablonski — Laws of UX** — Fitts, Hick, Jakob, aesthetic-usability.
  *Steal:* big/near targets, fewer choices, match conventions, polish buys
  forgiveness. https://lawsofux.com/

**Living platform canon:** Apple HIG (clarity, deference, depth) —
https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ · Google Material 3
(adaptive layout, dynamic color, spring motion) — https://m3.material.io/

**Taste anchors** (to locate a product on the two axes, not to copy): **Linear**
(quiet + dense), **Stripe** (developer-grade polish), **Things** (quiet + sparse),
**Teenage Engineering** (deliberate personality), **Figma / Notion / Arc**
(expression when earned).
